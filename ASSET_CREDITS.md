# Asset credits and licenses

Assets added for Ironbound are original work made with the scripts in `tools/`, so they
are released under the project's MIT license (see `LICENSE`). Rebuild them with the
commands below to change them. No assets from Beyond All Reason or other non-commercial
sources are used.

| Assets | Made by | How to rebuild |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `assets/models/ironbound/` (desert props, deposits, units, buildings) | modelled procedurally in Blender (bpy 5.0) by `tools/blender/build_assets.py`; the play-ready unit models (rifleman_squad, raider, scout_buggy_v2, tank, heavy_tank, artillery, missile_truck, helicopter) by the `tools/blender/build_*.py` scripts on `mesh_kit.py` | see `tools/blender/README.md` |
| `assets/models/ironbound/units/{battle_tank,rocket_technical,gunship}.glb`, `assets/models/ironbound/buildings/{bunker,flak_tower,gun_nest,sam_site,foundry,trading_post}.glb` (the faction models, 2026-10-06), `assets/models/ironbound/buildings/generated/*.glb` (the player buildings, 2026-10-06) | generated with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.5 concept image, then Tripo H3.1 image-to-3D) on the project owner's paid plan, then made play-ready (facing, size, team-colour split, one 1024 px base-colour texture, rotor blades on the gunship) by `tools/blender/clean_generated.py`. Higgsfield's Terms of Use (section 4.4) leave the outputs to the user, including commercial use and sublicensing, so they ship under the project's MIT license; the prompts are in `tools/blender/generated_models.md` | `tools/blender/generated_models.md` |
| `assets/models/ironbound/construction/city_build_up.glb` (starter city build-up: cranes, scaffold, workers, staged command centre, dust) | modelled and animated procedurally in Blender (bpy 5.0) by `tools/blender/build_construction.py` | `python3.11 tools/blender/build_construction.py` |

| `assets/audio/war/` (cannon, rifle and rocket fire, shell impacts, explosions, tank engine, rotor and drone loops) | synthesized from noise and sine waves by `tools/audio/make_war_sounds.py` | `python3 tools/audio/make_war_sounds.py` (needs numpy and ffmpeg) |

The music is rendered with the **GeneralUser GS v2.0.3** SoundFont by S. Christian Collins
(<https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser>). The SoundFont itself is not in the
repository: `make_music.py` downloads it from
<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/main/GeneralUser-GS.sf2>
(32 MB, sha256 `9575028c7a1f589f5770fccc8cff2734566af40cd26ed836944e9a5152688cfe`) into
`~/.cache/ironbound-music/`. Its license
(<https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS/main/documentation/LICENSE.txt>,
"License v2.0") reads in part: "You may use GeneralUser GS without restriction for your own
music creation, private or commercial. [...] Please feel free to use it in your software
projects, and to modify the SoundFont bank or its packaging to suit your needs." and "GeneralUser
GS inherits the usage rights of the samples contained within, all of which allow full use in
music production, including the ability to make profit from musical recordings created with
GeneralUser GS." The rendered tracks are therefore original recordings released under the
project's MIT license like the other generated assets.
